# 风水 / 方位 Reference

## Scope

Without floor plan, compass sitting/facing, construction period, and precise environment, do not claim full professional堪舆. Provide shape/form and symbolic direction analysis.

风水 output must be practical: reduce friction, strengthen attention/support, improve light/flow/privacy, and avoid expensive superstition.

## Method Lenses

Use hierarchy:

1. **形势为体**: backing, open front, left/right balance, door/window/traffic/sha, noise, clutter, visibility.
2. **八卦方位**: 乾坤震巽坎离艮兑 as symbolic mapping.
3. **Office power mapping**: boss position, manager behind/side/front, screen visibility, aisle pressure, meeting line of sight.
4. **理气为用**: 八宅/玄空飞星 only if period, sitting/facing degree, and floor plan are available.

No compass and no floor plan means no confident飞星 judgment.

High-quality Feng Shui readings do not start by selling cures. They start by checking what the body actually experiences: support behind, openness ahead, pressure lines, glare, noise, privacy, door flow, and whether the layout helps or leaks attention.

## Input Gate

Ask or infer:

- Home/office type and purpose.
- Sitting/facing degree, measured at least three times if possible.
- Door, window, aisle, desk, bed, stove, water, bathroom, trash, noise, stairs/elevator.
- Photos or floor plan if available.
- Construction/renovation period if using玄空.
- Main problem: health tendency, focus, boss support, sales, relationship, sleep, money flow.

## Master-Style Reading Order

1. Identify the core problem and the place where qi enters.
2. Check形势:
   - Behind: support, wall, door/window/traffic behind.
   - Front: open field, screen visibility, direct冲.
   - Left/right: support vs pressure, movement vs stability.
   - Sha: sharp corners, beams, direct doors, clutter, noise, glare.
3. Map eight directions:
   - 乾 northwest: authority, boss, rules, father/elder, official support.
   - 坤 southwest: support, platform, HR, mass resources.
   - 震 east: launch, movement, project trigger.
   - 巽 southeast: information, traffic, search, AI, writing, wind, hair.
   - 坎 north: risk, data flow, hidden issues.
   - 离 south: visibility, data, screen, reputation.
   - 艮 northeast: boundary, stop, team wall.
   - 兑 west: speech, negotiation, joy.
4. If玄空 inputs exist:
   - Establish 元运, 坐向, 山星/向星/运星.
   - Judge mountain star for people/health/support and water star for money/movement.
   - Tie star meaning back to actual form; star without form is weak.
5. Translate to action.

## Decisive Verdict Rules

- A seat with solid backing, clear front, and no direct冲 is materially better than a seat exposed from behind.
- If the boss or key authority is visually behind/above/pressing the user, read it as pressure unless there is strong support elsewhere.
- If the user faces clutter/noise/traffic, read it as attention leakage before mystical symbolism.
- If a claimed cure does not change light, flow, privacy, noise, cleanliness, or behavior, treat it as weak symbolic support.
- If form and飞星 conflict, prioritize form for immediate lived effect; use飞星 for longer-period emphasis.
- If the reading is for office politics or promotion, map leaders and movement lines as environmental pressure/support first, then read direction symbolism second.

## Office Reading Order

1. Determine user's facing degree.
2. Map key people/objects:
   - Direct manager.
   - Higher boss.
   - Door/window/aisle.
   - Printer/trash/noise/traffic.
   - Blank wall or support behind.
3. Read power relation:
   - Behind with support: protection, sponsorship, stability.
   - Behind with surveillance/traffic: pressure, unease, loss of control.
   - In front: visibility and scrutiny.
   - At side: collaboration or distraction depending on movement/noise.
4. Give adjustment in low-cost order:
   - clean and remove clutter.
   - improve backing and screen privacy.
   - soften direct冲.
   - shift seat angle if possible.
   - use plant/light/water only when practical and safe.

## Limits

- Do not promise wealth, health, or promotion solely from layout.
- Do not recommend structural renovation without clear need.
- Do not overfit: a good seat helps attention and symbolic support; it does not replace work output, boss sponsorship, or process.
